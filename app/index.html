    About Beneath

    Beneath is an explorable globe of what lies under the Earth's surface. Drag the depth slider to peel back the surface, then click or tap anywhere to read what the data says about that point.

    This site is educational. It is not an exploration, engineering or investment tool. Data resolution is kilometres, not metres, and some datasets are decades old.
